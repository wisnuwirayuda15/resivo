---
title: Local first
description: Your resumes live in your browser on your device, with no account and no copy elsewhere, so a backup is the one thing that protects them.
---

Resivo keeps everything you write in your own browser, on the device you wrote
it on. There is no account, no server holding your resumes and no sync. That is
the reason for most of the choices in the app, and it has consequences worth
knowing before you rely on it.

## What is stored, and where [#storage]

- Your resumes, groups, images and fonts are stored in your browser's
  IndexedDB, a database that belongs to this site in this browser.
- A few preferences are stored in the browser's local storage: the interface
  language, the colour theme, whether the sidebar is collapsed, and which tours
  you have seen.

Nothing else about you is kept. Resivo has no sign-in, so there is no profile.

## What leaves your device [#network]

No resume content is ever sent anywhere. Loading Resivo is an ordinary request
for the app's files, as with any website, and the server that answers sees what
any web server sees, such as your network address. What you type, the images you
add and the files you export never form part of a request.

The documentation works the same way. Search runs in your browser against an
index it downloads once. **Ask an assistant** on a docs page opens another site
only when you choose it, and sends only the address of that public page.

## The tradeoff [#tradeoff]

<Callout type="warning" title="The only copy is on your device">

Clearing your browser's site data deletes your resumes, and so does losing the
device. Nobody can recover them for you, because nobody else has them.

</Callout>

The editor says this in its save indicator: **Saved** means _written to this
device_, and that there is no copy anywhere else.

## Back up [#backup]

In **Settings**, **Backup and restore** protects against that. **Download
backup** writes every resume, group, image and font on the device to one JSON
file, which your browser saves. Keep it somewhere that is not this device.

**Restore** adds a backup's contents to the device and never replaces or deletes
what is already there. A resume that collides with one you already have is
restored beside it, with `(restored)` added to its name, and an image already
stored is not stored twice.

To move one resume rather than everything, use **Export as zip** on its card, or
**Bundle (.zip)** in the editor's **Export** menu, and import the file from the
**New resume** dialog on the other device.

## Working offline [#offline]

Resivo can be installed like an app, and once it has cached itself it opens with
no network at all. **Settings** says which of these is true on your device,
under **Install on this device**.
