## Do I need an account? [#account]

No. There is no sign-in and no profile. See [Privacy](/docs/data/privacy).

## Where are my resumes kept? [#where]

In your browser, on your device, and nowhere else. See
[Local first](/docs/getting-started/local-first).

## Can I use it on two devices? [#devices]

Not by syncing: there is no server to sync through. Move a resume with a bundle, or
the whole device with a backup. See [Bundles](/docs/export/bundles) and
[Backup and restore](/docs/data/backup-restore).

## What if I clear my browser? [#cleared]

Your resumes go with it, and there is no other copy. A backup is the only thing
that survives. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting#gone).

## Does it work offline? [#offline]

Yes, once the app has cached itself. See
[Install and work offline](/docs/data/install-offline).

## Why Markdown? [#markdown]

A resume is mostly structure, and Markdown is plain text that says it. It can be
written by hand, kept in version control or produced by an assistant, and every
change on the paper is written back into it. See
[The shape of the file](/docs/format/overview).

## Can an AI write my resume? [#ai]

An assistant can, and these docs have prompts that teach one the format and tell it
never to invent facts. Nothing in Resivo sends your resume to one. See
[AI prompts](/docs/format/ai-prompts) and [Privacy](/docs/data/privacy#assistants).

## Is the PDF readable by a tracking system? [#pdf]

It is made by printing, so it has real text and not a picture of text. Every
template is a single column. The ATS tab looks for other things that may trip a
parser, and cannot promise how any one system reads a file. See
[The ATS check](/docs/checks/ats-check) and [PDF](/docs/export/pdf).

## Can I use my own font? [#fonts]

Yes, with a WOFF2, WOFF, TrueType or OpenType file. It is embedded in an HTML
export. A system that re-draws the text may use a face of its own, so the built-in
faces are the safer choice for a parser. See
[Images and fonts](/docs/design/images-and-fonts).

## Can I change how a template looks? [#style]

Yes, by the **Style** tab first and by your own CSS second. See
[The style inspector](/docs/design/style-inspector) and
[Custom CSS](/docs/design/custom-css).

## Can I tailor a resume for each job? [#jobs]

Make a version for each company, and see what you changed with **Compare**. See
[Versions for a job](/docs/jobs/versions).

## Can I import a resume I already have? [#import]

Yes, from Markdown, JSON Resume, plain text or a bundle. See
[Import formats](/docs/export/import-formats).

## Can I read these docs in another language? [#docs-language]

In English and Indonesian, at the same address with `/en` or `/id`. See
[Language](/docs/help/language).
