**Settings** is in the sidebar. It holds the questions about Resivo that are not
about any one resume. There are four sections, in this order.

## Language [#language]

The language of menus, dialogs and messages. It is one setting, **Interface
language**, and it applies the moment you choose. It does not translate what you
write. See [Language](/docs/help/language).

## Backup and restore [#backup]

**Download backup** writes everything on the device to one file, and **Restore**
adds a backup's contents back. This is the one section worth visiting before you
need it. See [Backup and restore](/docs/data/backup-restore).

## Storage [#storage]

How many images and fonts are stored, how much room they take, and how many nothing
uses. Each line leads to its page. See [Storage](/docs/data/storage).

## Install on this device [#install]

Whether Resivo can be installed, whether it is, and whether the app is cached so it
opens with no network. See
[Install and work offline](/docs/data/install-offline).

## What is not here [#not-here]

A few things that look like settings are somewhere else, because they belong to
what you are doing.

- The colour theme is a button in the bar at the top of every page, and in the
  application menu on a narrow screen.
- A resume's own settings, such as its template, its fonts and the language of its
  dates, are in the **Style** tab of the editor. See
  [The style inspector](/docs/design/style-inspector).
- The size of the editor's panes is set by dragging, and remembered.
- The sidebar is collapsed with its toggle, and remembered.
