title: The tours
description: The two short tours that run the first time you open the library and the editor, what each points out, how to skip one, and how to take it again.
---

The first time you open the library and the first time you open the editor, a short
tour points out what a new person would otherwise miss. Each is a few steps, each
step is a card beside the part of the screen it describes, and neither blocks you:
you can skip it at any point.

## Moving through a tour [#use]

Each card has **Next**, **Prev**, **Skip** and, on the last step, **End**. Skipping
or ending a tour counts as having seen it, and it does not run again on its own.

On a narrow screen the editor shows one pane at a time. The steps that describe a
pane then point at the tab that opens it, and the tour opens the pane it is
describing.

## The library tour [#library]

- **Start here** points at **New resume**: pick a single-column template, or
  import a file you already have. The importer keeps what it cannot typeset and
  does not drop it.
- **Images and fonts are shared** points at the sidebar's **Images** and **Fonts**:
  uploaded once, available to every resume, and showing what nothing uses.
- **This is the important one** points at **Settings**: everything lives in this
  browser, and a backup is the only thing that survives it being cleared.
- **Everything, from the keyboard** points at the application menu and says that
  Ctrl+K, or Cmd+K on a Mac, opens the command palette.

## The editor tour [#editor]

- **Markdown, and your own CSS** points at the **Code** pane, with its two tabs.
- **Every directive, with an example** points at the Guide button in the Code
  pane, which opens the documentation on the format.
- **The paper is editable too** points at **Visual** mode, where you click text to
  change it and drag blocks to move them.
- **Export is the same document** points at the export controls: the HTML is one
  self-contained file, the PDF is that file printed, and the Markdown uses the
  editor's own writer.
- **Four tabs worth knowing** points at the **Style** pane's tabs.
- **Nothing here is one-way** points at undo and redo.

## Taking a tour again [#again]

Open the application menu in the bar at the top and choose **Take the tour**. It
starts the tour for the page you are on, the library's in the library and the
editor's in the editor. It runs even if you have seen it.

## What is remembered [#remembered]

Whether you have seen each tour is kept in the browser's local storage, on this
device. A private window, or cleared site data, shows the tours again, which is
right: to Resivo that is a new device. See [Storage](/docs/data/storage).
