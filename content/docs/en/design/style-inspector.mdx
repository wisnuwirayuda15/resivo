The **Style** pane on the right of the editor is the inspector. It does not write
Markdown. It changes everything about a resume that is not its content: how it
looks, which section is where, which pictures and fonts it uses, and whether a
parser will read it well. On a narrow screen it is the **Style** tab along the
top of the editor. See [The editor](/docs/editor/overview).

## The four tabs [#tabs]

- **Style** holds the template and every design setting: paper, type, colour,
  spacing, rules, icons, photo, page breaks and language.
- **Sections** is the outline and the contacts. Each section can be moved, hidden,
  given an icon, or set to start on a new page, and each contact can have an icon
  and a link.
- **Assets** holds the images and fonts stored on this device, and is where you
  place one in a resume. See [Images and fonts](/docs/design/images-and-fonts).
- **ATS** reads the resume the way an applicant tracking system might and lists
  what could go wrong.

The **Sections** tab is where a section's **Start on a new page** is, which is
one of the ways of ending a page. See
[Pages and breaks](/docs/editor/pages-and-breaks#section).

## A change is an edit [#edits]

Moving a control writes to the resume exactly as typing does. The paper
re-flows, the change is saved by autosave and the undo button can take it back.
Nothing in the inspector tells the preview to update: the preview draws again
because the resume changed.

Dragging a slider or holding down a stepper makes a stream of changes, and they
are joined into one undo step, so undo takes back the size change and not one
tenth of a point of it. A switch or a menu is a step of its own. See
[Autosave and undo](/docs/editor/autosave-and-undo).

## The Style tab [#style]

The tab starts with the **Template** group, then runs down through **Paper**,
**Typography**, **Colour**, **Spacing**, **Rules**, **Icons**, **Photo**,
**Page breaks** and **Language**. A control that is not obvious has a line of
help under it. Every control, its range and the CSS variable it sets are
listed in [Design tokens](/docs/design/design-tokens).

### Try the Style tab first [#first]

Most styling here is not CSS. There is a control for every setting the templates
read, and moving one is both easier and safer than overriding it in CSS: the
paper is measured at the values you set, so the pages stay right. Reach for
[Custom CSS](/docs/design/custom-css) only for what no control can do.
