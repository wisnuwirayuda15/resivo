---
title: Visual editing
description: Edit text directly on the paper, drag and reorder blocks and sections, delete a block, and change an image's width.
---

Besides writing Markdown, you can edit on the paper itself. At the top of the
**Paper** pane, **Preview mode** switches between two modes:

- **Read** shows the page and nothing else. It is what you export.
- **Visual** makes the page editable.

Visual mode changes what you can do, not what the page is. The controls it draws
sit in the margin and are never part of what is measured, so turning it on
cannot move a page break.

## Edit text [#text]

In **Visual** mode, click a piece of text on the paper to edit it. You can also
move to it with the Tab key and press Enter. Each field is edited on its own:
you change the words in that one place, not the shape of the document.

- Press Enter, or click elsewhere, to keep the change.
- Press Escape to discard it.

Whatever you keep is written back into the Markdown, so the code pane shows it
at once. The change is one step in the undo history, and while you are typing in
a field the browser's own undo applies to that field's typing.

## Move things [#move]

Hover over a block or a section heading and a small set of controls appears in
the margin:

- **Drag to move** is a handle you drag.
- **Move up** and **Move down** do the same one place at a time, and are the way
  to do it from the keyboard.
- **Delete** removes a block. It is a block-level control: a section is
  removed from the **Sections** tab, where it asks first.

The header at the top of the page has no controls, since it has nowhere to move
to.

What a move means depends on what you drop on what:

- A block dropped on another block takes its place, in the same section or a
  different one.
- A block dropped on a section heading goes to the top of that section.
- A section heading dropped on another section heading reorders the sections.

Any other combination does nothing, rather than a guess. Every move can be
undone.

## Images [#images]

An image has **Image width** among its controls. The width is a percentage of
the text column, and is what the `width` in the Markdown directive holds.

## Page breaks [#breaks]

**Insert a page break after this** adds a page break after a block. See
[Pages and breaks](/docs/editor/pages-and-breaks).
