---
title: Organize your resumes
description: Open, search, sort, group, duplicate, archive and delete resumes in the library, and what each of those does to the resume.
---

The library is the grid of every resume you have. Each card shows a small
picture of the paper, the resume's name, when it was last edited and which
template it uses. Choose a card to open it in the editor.

## Search and sort [#search]

**Search resumes** at the top of the library filters as you type. It looks at a
resume's name, the name and headline in its header, and, for a version made for a
job, the company and role. Searching for "acme" finds the resume written for
Acme whatever it is called. It ignores capital letters and matches any part of
those words. If nothing matches, the library says **No matches**.

**Sort resumes** orders what is shown by **Last edited**, which is the default,
**Date created** or **Name**.

The search, the sort and the group you are looking at are kept in the address of
the page, so a filtered view can be bookmarked and survives a reload.

## Groups [#groups]

Groups are your own folders. In the sidebar, **New group** makes one, and the
**Groups** list shows them, each opening the resumes in it. **All resumes** shows
everything, and a resume in no group appears under **Ungrouped**.

A resume is in at most one group. Put it in one when you make it, or later with
**Move to** in its card menu.

From the menu beside a group you can **Rename** it or **Delete group**. Deleting
a group deletes nothing but the group: the resumes in it become ungrouped.

## The card menu [#menu]

The **More** button appears at the corner of a card when you hover over it or
move to it with the keyboard. Its menu has:

- **Rename** changes the resume's name.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy named after the original with `copy` added, in the
  same group. The copy is independent: editing one never changes the other.
- **Create version for a job** copies the resume for one company and role.
- **Create cover letter** makes a letter from the resume. It is not offered on a
  letter.
- **Export as zip** writes the resume with its images and fonts as one file.
- **Move to** files it in a group.
- **Archive** and **Delete**, described next.

Cover letters sit in the same library as resumes. **Cover letters** in the
sidebar shows only the letters, and a letter's card says **Cover letter**.

## Archive [#archive]

**Archive** takes a resume out of the library without deleting it. Archived
resumes are listed under **Archived** in the sidebar, and **Restore** in a card's
menu puts one back. Archive what you are not using now and may want again.

## Delete [#delete]

**Delete** asks first. Deleting removes the resume from this device and it
cannot be recovered, because there is no other copy. If you only want it out
of the way, archive it instead. If you might need it on another device, export it
as a zip or [back up](/docs/getting-started/local-first) first.
